# Supermarket-Sales-Analysis-part2
## Projective objective
The core goal is to analyze supermarket sales data in Excel and build an interactive dashboard that presents key business metrics, category-wise sales contributions, outlet comparisons, and statistical insights.
## Data used
-<a href="https://github.com/singhdarshan3417-arch/Supermarket-Sales-Analysis-Answers/blob/main/Supermarket_Sales_Analysis_Answers%20(1).xlsx">Dataset</a>
## Questions
•  What is the total sales revenue generated by the supermarket?
•  What is the average sales value per record?
•  What is the average visibility of products across the supermarket?
•  What percentage of total sales is contributed by the top four product categories compared with all remaining categories?
•  Which product categories generate the highest and lowest total sales?
•  How do total sales compare across different outlet types?
•  How do sales vary across outlets for Low Fat and Regular products?
•  Is there a relationship between product visibility and sales, and what trend can be observed?
•  How do sales contributions from different product categories vary across outlet types?
•  What are the average, median, minimum, and maximum sales values?
•  How much variation exists in sales across product records?
•  Which records have unusually high or low sales compared with most records?
•  How does average sales performance differ across product categories, outlet types, and outlet locations?
## Process
Understand the dataset

Identify columns such as sales, product category, outlet type, product visibility and fat content.



Clean the data

Remove duplicates, handle missing values, correct data types and check inconsistent entries.



Perform data analysis

Calculate total sales, average sales, category contributions and outlet performance.



Perform statistical analysis

Calculate mean, median, minimum, maximum, standard deviation and identify unusual sales values.



Create charts and PivotTables

Use Excel PivotTables, PivotCharts and formulas to summarize the results.



Build an interactive dashboard

Add KPI cards, charts, slicers and filters.



Interpret the results

Write insights and recommendations based on the actual data.
